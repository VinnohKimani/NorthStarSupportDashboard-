# Problem Definition

## Northstar Support Deflection Dashboard — MVP

### Client

Northstar Retail Co. is a mid-size e-commerce company whose support team receives repetitive customer questions.

The purpose of this MVP is to reduce manual handling of these repetitive questions by allowing customers to find answers themselves.

## Selected Problems

For the MVP, we will focus on two support problems:

1. Order Status
2. Stock Availability

---

## Problem 1: Order Status

### Customer Problem

Customers contact Northstar support to ask questions such as:

- Where is my order?
- Has my order shipped?
- When will my order arrive?

### MVP Solution

The customer enters their Order ID into the self-service dashboard.

The system retrieves the relevant order information and displays:

- Current order status
- Latest order update
- Expected delivery date

### Desired Outcome

The customer can determine the status of their order without contacting a support agent.

---

## Problem 2: Stock Availability

### Customer Problem

Customers contact Northstar support to ask questions such as:

- Is this product available?
- Do you have this product in my size?
- When will this size be available again?

### MVP Solution

The customer searches for a product and selects the required size or variation.

The system checks the product and stock data and displays:

- Availability
- Stock quantity where appropriate
- Expected restock date where available

### Desired Outcome

The customer can determine product/size availability without contacting a support agent.

---

## Why These Two Problems?

We selected Order Status and Stock Availability because both can be demonstrated using structured data within the one-week MVP sprint.

The objective is to demonstrate the support-deflection concept end-to-end rather than build a production-ready system.
